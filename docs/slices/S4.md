# S4: Saved library

Goal: save a video from an idea or while watching, find it again later without a check-in, and stay inside YouTube's 30-day rule for stored details.

**Files (under ``):** `src/lib/{saved,use-saved,actions-client}.ts`, `src/actions/saved.ts`, `src/components/SaveButton.tsx`, `src/pages/(app)/(protected)/saved.tsx`, edits to `PickCard`, `WatchPanel`, `checkin.tsx`, `home.tsx`, `nav.ts`, `tests/saved.spec.ts`.

**In scope:** Save / Saved toggle on idea cards and the watch screen (instant, then confirmed by the server); Saved page with category filter, "How to do it", your own note (saved on blur), Watch, Open on YouTube, Remove with confirmation; videos that can't play are marked and the mark is remembered ("No longer on YouTube", "Can't play here"); stored YouTube details refreshed from day 25 (`refreshSaved` action, up to 5 per call, once per browser session) and **withheld** from display after day 30 without a refresh; a removed video's stored YouTube details are cleared and the person's note is kept.

**Out of scope:** scheduled (cron) refresh, sharing, collections/folders.

**Acceptance (automated):** unit tests for the 25/30-day rules, display rules, and the refresh action (stale only, never marks gone on a failed lookup, keeps the note, caps work per call); 4 browser tests on the real local database: save persists across reloads and sign-in sessions, a second account cannot see it (fails if member read is opened up), double-tap keeps one row, notes persist, filter, remove, direct access with no check-in, unplayable-video marks persist.

**What these checks do NOT prove:** the refresh against real YouTube (the integration was failing upstream; "success with an empty list means removed" is an assumption); the withheld-details display in a browser (unit-tested only); real video playback from the library.

**Decision recorded:** writes the person must not lose (save, remove, notes, delete check-in) use the SDK's confirmed variants, because the plain ones are fire-and-forget and a quick navigation could drop them.
