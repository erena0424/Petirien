# S6: Talk to the bunny, and a journal it writes (decided 2026-10-01)

**Elena's vision:** For people who want to document what they think and go through but don't like journaling. They talk to the bunny; the bunny replies and gives gentle feedback; the bunny writes a summary that serves as the journal entry. It doubles as a reflection activity. The main button becomes "Do something little for yourself". The chat lives on Home by default so a person can say one small thing whenever it comes to mind and does not have to hold a full conversation.

**Elena's answers:** keep **summary only** (not the chat); the entry is written as **the bunny's notes about you** ("You told me..."); chat on Home by default, "Do something little for yourself" stays as the button; **reflection first, then deploy**.

**Defaults chosen by the coding agent (Elena can change):**
- Bunny mostly listens, says back what it heard, at most one gentle question, sometimes a small perspective. No diagnosis, no medical or medication advice, no "you must".
- 1 to 3 short sentences per reply. Plain voice, no lists, no emoji, no exclamation marks, no long dashes.
- Nothing is stored until the person presses **Save to my journal**, sees a preview of the bunny's notes, and presses **Save**. Discard throws it away. The chat itself is never stored.
- The bunny does not remember earlier chats (v1). Each chat starts fresh.
- A saved entry counts as a day on the calendar.
- Text only. No voice.
- Crisis checks on **every** message (phrase list, then the model's own flag). A hit shows the support card and the bunny stops replying.
- Cap: 80 bunny replies or summaries per person per day. The app owner is exempt.
- The chat says plainly that the bunny is an AI and not a therapist, and that nothing is saved unless you save the notes.

**Data flow to disclose (privacy page):** while chatting, the messages are sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude via DeepSpace) on every turn. Only the saved summary is stored.

**Files (under ``):** `src/reflect/*`, `src/actions/reflect.ts`, `journalEntries` collection, `src/lib/use-journal.ts`, `src/components/ChatBox.tsx`, `src/pages/(app)/(protected)/journal.tsx`, edits to Home, nav, calendar, privacy, delete-everything.

**What the checks will NOT prove:** that the real model's replies are good in tone (needs a real account with credits; test accounts have none); how replies feel over a longer chat.
