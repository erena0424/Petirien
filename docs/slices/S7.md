# S7: Saved conversations, a Messages tab, and a floating bunny (decided 2026-10-01)

**Elena's direction (replaces the S6 "summary only" rule):**
- Conversations with the bunny are **saved by default**, with a **"Don't save this chat"** option. A Messages tab shows conversations like a texting app and lets you start a new one.
- The landing page has no chat card: a **very big bunny** with its words next to it. By default the greeting ("Good afternoon. Say something..."); once you talk, its **latest reply**.
- The bunny and the text box **float**, can be **dragged**, come in **three sizes**, and a tap **expands** them to show earlier messages. The bunny can appear on **any page**.
- The AI disclaimer sits at the **bottom** of the chat, not directly under the text box.
- The bunny writes **small notes automatically** so context is not lost. They are visible in the Journal and used to personalize replies.
- Build order: **saved chats and Messages tab, then the floating bunny, then memory**.

**Decided (Elena, 2026-10-01): the bunny remembers tone and the kind of experience a person likes, automatically, and never confidential content.** Built as a **fixed vocabulary only** (`src/reflect/style.ts`): tone (gentle, upbeat, direct, playful), reply length (short, longer), questions (fewer, more), suggestions (fewer, more). Every field is an enum, so there is nowhere to store anything the person said or any life detail; anything outside the list is dropped. The bunny picks it up on its own when the person says how they want it to talk ("shorter please"), and it is **visible and editable** under Preferences ("How the bunny talks with you"), with Forget all. It is stored on the person's own preferences row. Nothing is learned from a crisis conversation.

**Data model:**
- `conversations`: userId, title (from the first message), lastMessageAt, notedUpTo (how many messages the notes already cover).
- `messages`: userId, conversationId, role, text, seq.
- `journalEntries` gains `conversationId` and `auto` so an automatic note can be traced to its chat.
- A "don't save this chat" conversation creates **no rows at all**; it lives only in the page.
- Owner-only for every role (admin sees own rows only), included in Delete everything, and in the privacy page.

**Personalization (now):** each bunny turn also receives up to the last five journal notes (title and notes only) as background. Sent to the same AI service; disclosed on the privacy page.

**Automatic notes:** triggered by the client, written by a server action that reads the stored messages of a conversation the caller owns (never trusting the client's text), covers only messages not yet noted, one per page load at most, only after the chat has been quiet for 30 minutes or the person starts a new one. A failure changes nothing; it is retried later because the messages are saved.

**Safety (unchanged and stricter):** crisis checks on every message, no model call when they fire, support card inline, the daily cap, the AI disclosure.

**Not proven by tests:** the real model's tone; whether the automatic notes read well.

## Floating bunny: requirements gathered so far (phase 2, not built)
- Big bunny with its latest words, and the text box, **float** on every page. Draggable, three sizes, tap to expand.
- Expanded view shows the **latest conversation large and a few earlier conversations much smaller** beneath it, so it feels like a text thread and past chats are still within reach.
- The **text box grows as the person types** (up to a limit, then scrolls) and shrinks after sending. (Done already on Home and Messages.)
- AI disclaimer at the bottom of the panel, not directly under the text box (footer already says "The bunny is an AI").
- Must never cover the "Need support now?" link, must be usable by keyboard and screen reader, and must stop moving for people who turn off motion. Phone: docked, not draggable over content.
